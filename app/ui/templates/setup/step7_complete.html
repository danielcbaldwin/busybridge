Getting Started

1

Select Your Main Calendar

Go to Settings and choose which calendar should receive all synced events. This is usually your primary work calendar.

2

Connect Client Calendars

From the dashboard, connect calendars from your client organizations. You'll sign into each client's Google account and select which calendar to sync.

3

Let It Sync

Events sync automatically every 5 minutes, with near-instant sync via webhooks for most changes. You can also trigger a manual sync from the dashboard.
